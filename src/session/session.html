Capture Kit

Capture a session

Pick the tabs you are working across. Each one is captured in turn, and you get a single folder with an index that names the console errors and network failures on every page — the file you hand your AI assistant.

Without this, tabs opened before Capture Kit started will say their console data is unavailable rather than pretending they were clean. Reloading loses anything unsaved on the page.

While this runs, the browser moves through your tabs one at a time. Chrome only draws the tab you are looking at, so each one has to come to the front to be captured.
