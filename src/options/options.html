Image format for reports JPEG keeps files small enough to share. PNG is pixel-exact and much larger. "Image only" has its own PNG switch in the popup.

Automation

Which pages can trigger a capture An AI assistant driving this browser can capture without a keystroke by dispatching capture-kit:capture (or capture-kit:record for a clip) on the page and listening for capture-kit:capture-complete. A page that fires one brings its tab to the front and writes a file into your output folder, so choose where that is allowed.
One per line. localhost:3000 allows one port; localhost allows any port; *.example.com allows a domain and all its subdomains. Scheme and path are ignored.
